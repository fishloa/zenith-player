- Subtitle: Live TV from your own server

## Description

Zenith Player plays live TV channels from an M3U playlist you provide. Point it
at your own TV server, or let it find a Zenith server on your home network.

- Channel grid grouped by country or category, with logos
- Programme guide from your playlist's XMLTV guide
- On Apple TV, the focused channel plays live behind the grid
- Picks the right frame rate and dynamic range for each channel
- Multiple audio tracks
- Your playlist address syncs across your devices with iCloud

Zenith Player includes no channels or content. You need a playlist of MPEG-TS
streams that you are entitled to watch.

Zenith Player is open source.
