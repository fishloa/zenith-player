# Zenith Player privacy policy

Zenith Player does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
- It connects only to the playlist, stream, logo and guide addresses you
  configure, or to a Zenith server it finds on your local network.
- Your playlist address is stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, in
  your iCloud key-value storage so your other devices use the same
  playlist. It is never sent to the developer.
- Playback diagnostics stay on the device.

Questions: open an issue at the project's GitHub repository.
